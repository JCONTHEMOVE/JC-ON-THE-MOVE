Here’s the clearest projection: JC ON THE MOVE should become more than a moving company website. It should become a worker-powered booking, dispatch, payout, rewards, and referral platform.
Right now, the site can evolve into three connected experiences:
1. Customer-Facing Site
This should be simple, fast, and conversion-focused.
The first screen should make it instantly clear:
JC ON THE MOVE
Moving. Junk Removal. Delivery. Cleanup.
Then the customer should be able to:
Get an estimate
Book a job
Upload photos
Choose service type
See trust signals
Pay or authorize payment
Track job status
Leave a review
The marketing angle should be direct:
“Fast local help for moving, hauling, delivery, and cleanup.”

This should feel practical, local, trustworthy, and easy. No clutter. No confusing menus. The customer should always know what to do next.
2. Worker / Crew Experience
This is where the site becomes powerful.
Workers should have their own simple dashboard showing:
Today’s jobs
Assigned crew
Job address
Hours worked
Role on job
Estimated pay
Bonus opportunity
Completed jobs
Total earned
JCMOVES rewards
Referral link
This matters because workers are not just labor. They become part of the marketing engine.
Each worker should eventually get a shareable page/link like:
jconthemove.com/rep/matt
That page can show:
Their name
Services they can help book
Referral code
Booking button
Earnings/rewards opportunity
Trust badge: “JC ON THE MOVE verified worker”
This makes every worker a marketer.
3. Admin Command Center
This is the business cockpit.
Admin should be able to see:
Leads
Booked jobs
Assigned workers
Revenue
Labor cost
Expenses
Net profit
Profit per labor hour
Worker payouts
Referral payouts
Rewards issued
Job status
The main business metric should stay exactly what we discussed:
Profit Per Labor Hour
That tells you if the business is actually getting stronger job by job.
The admin experience should eventually feel like:
“I can see every job, every dollar, every worker, every referral, and every payout from one place.”

That is the north star.
What It Should Look Like
Visually, I would push the site toward a clean, strong local-service brand:
White / black / green / gold accents
Real job photos
Bold service buttons
Mobile-first layout
Worker cards
Simple dashboards
Clear money numbers
No overly decorative clutter
Strong calls to action
The customer side should feel trustworthy and quick.
The worker side should feel like opportunity.
The admin side should feel like control.
How To Get There
I would build this in phases:
Phase 1: Make the Live Site Convert
Focus on customers first.
Tighten homepage messaging
Make booking obvious
Add service pages for moving, junk removal, delivery, cleanup
Add photo upload estimate flow
Add trust/review sections
Add clear phone/text buttons
Make mobile perfect
Phase 2: Make Jobs Operational
This is where the business becomes systemized.
Lead to booked job flow
Job status tracking
Crew assignments
Hours worked
Customer approval
Payment status
Admin job dashboard
Phase 3: Make Worker Pay Automatic
This is the payout system we integrated.
Guaranteed hourly pay
Profit-sharing preview
Net job profit
Crew bonus pool
Referral payout
Growth fund
Manual payout status
Admin override for unprofitable jobs
No automatic payout should happen until:
Job Status = Customer Approved
That rule protects the company.
Phase 4: Turn Workers Into Marketers
This is the growth layer.
Worker referral pages
Referral codes
Worker leaderboard
Commission tracking
“Book with this rep” links
Social-share assets
QR codes for flyers/business cards
This lets every worker bring in jobs and get rewarded.
Phase 5: Add Automation
Once the manual system works cleanly:
Stripe Connect payouts
Referral partner payouts
JCMOVES rewards ledger
SMS notifications
Email automation
Review requests
Customer follow-ups
Performance dashboards
Do not automate messy processes too early. First make the manual flow reliable, then automate it.
My Recommended Direction
The site should become:
A booking site for customers.
A money dashboard for workers.
A profit machine for admin.
A referral engine for growth.
That is the cleanest version of what we have been building.
The next practical move should be to polish the live customer journey and worker marketing journey together:
Homepage sells the service.
Booking flow captures jobs.
Admin assigns workers.
Workers see jobs and pay.
Customers approve completed work.
Admin finalizes payout.
Workers and referral partners get rewarded.
Everyone has a reason to bring in the next job.
